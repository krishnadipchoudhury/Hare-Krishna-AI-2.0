Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or email, we store your email address, display name, and profile photo (if provided) to identify your account and let you access your data across sessions and devices.

Your chats

Chat messages are stored securely with your account (via Firebase) so you can access them across sessions. If you use the app as a guest without signing in, your chats are stored only on your own device and are not sent to our servers for storage.

Memory

Anything you save to the app's Memory feature is stored with your account and used only to personalize your experience within this app. It is never shared with third parties or used for any purpose outside the app.

Web search

When the app searches the web to answer a question, your question text is sent to our search provider (Tavily) and our AI provider (Groq) solely to generate a response. These providers do not receive your name, email, or account information as part of that request.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to anyone. Deleted chats are moved to a "Recently deleted" area and kept for 14 days before being permanently removed. Chats shared via a link are visible to anyone who has that link.

Your choices

You can delete individual chats, clear your saved Memory, or delete your account data at any time from within the app's Settings. If you'd like your account fully removed, contact us using the email below.
